## Human review queue

For each record include:

- Lead ID and business identity
- Candidate domains and scores
- Why the result is inconclusive
- Queries and sources checked
- The smallest next action that could resolve it

## Method and limitations

- Search evidence can be stale or incomplete.
- Identity scoring verifies likely ownership; it does not prove website quality.
- A missing search result is not proof that no website exists.
- Contact data, if requested, should be reviewed before outreach.
- Outreach must follow applicable privacy, platform, and anti-spam rules.
